User need and requirements

Why a fall band

  • About one in four adults over 65 falls each year (CDC).
  • The danger is not the fall itself but lying alone afterwards for hours.
  • So the band must: notice the fall, alarm nearby, and escalate if nobody responds.

Hardware

  • B-L4S5I-IOT01A, Cortex-M4 at 80 MHz
  • LSM6DSL accelerometer + gyroscope (on board, I²C)
  • LED2, user button, UART1 at 115200
  • Grove buzzer on D6 (only add-on)

这是完整数据流。LSM6DSL 每 20 毫秒给三轴加速度和三轴角速度。汇编 EWMA 每个周期调用六次，每个轴有自己的 old output，存在两个长度为 3 的数组里。过滤后压成四个有物理意义的特征，交给五状态检测器，最后输出到 LED、蜂鸣器、串口和 Telegram。

两点要强调：进入检测器的只有汇编的输出，C 版本只做逐轴验证；数据是单向的，enhancement 只读状态，不会写回检测器。

Filter design: EWMA

y[n] = (α·x[n] + (100 − α)·y[n−1]) / 100
  • Signed integer arithmetic, α given as a percentage (25).
  • Division truncates toward zero, the same as C.
  • Only the previous output is stored: no sample buffer.
  • Time constant ≈ 1/α = 4 samples = 80 ms at 50 Hz. Desk jitter is smoothed; an impact of 100 to 200 ms still comes through.
  • Larger α: more noise passes. Smaller α: the impact is blurred.

Open test case 1 is a step from 0 to 1000. After 4 samples the output is 684, about two thirds of the way; it reaches 1000 by sample 15 with no overshoot.

EWMA 只需要上一次的输出，不需要 buffer。公式就是 alpha 乘新值加 100 减 alpha 乘旧值再除以 100，全部整数运算。我们用 alpha 25，时间常数大约 4 个样本、80 毫秒：静止时的抖动被压掉，但一两百毫秒的冲击仍然能过来。alpha 太大噪声过多，太小冲击会被抹平。

右图是 open test case 1，输入是 0 到 1000 的阶跃。4 个样本后输出到 684，大约三分之二，这就是时间常数的意思；到第 15 个样本接近 1000，没有过冲。

Assembly implementation and verification

    MOVS r3, #100
    MUL  r4, r0, r2        @ alpha * new
    SUB  r5, r3, r2        @ 100 - alpha
    MUL  r6, r5, r1        @ (100 - alpha) * old

0:45 · Xu Ruijie

函数按 ARM calling convention：r0、r1、r2 收 new data、old output、alpha，结果放回 r0。r3 只放常数 100，是 scratch 寄存器不用保存。我们用了 r4 到 r7，所以进函数 PUSH、出函数 POP；r8 到 r11 和浮点都没碰，也没有调用任何 C helper。

SDIV 是有符号除法，向零截断，和 C 的整数除法一样，负数输入也不会出错。测试工程五个 alpha、每组 16 个样本、三个轴，一共 240 个输出全部和 C 一致。固件里每次采样也把汇编和 C 的结果逐轴比较，不一致才会打印 WARNING，所有板上日志里从来没出现过这一行。

检测器有五个状态、三道门。NORMAL 里加速度低于 500 或高于 2000 mg 才进 SUSPECT。1 秒内要看到超过 200 dps 的旋转才进 CONFIRMING，否则回 NORMAL。CONFIRMING 至少等 2 秒让动作稳定，然后要求倾角大于 60 度并且 still，才进 FALL；5 秒内没躺稳也回 NORMAL。这两条橙色的回路就是 near fall，不报警。

FALL 是锁存的，只有蓝色按钮能清掉，所以不会闪烁。30 秒没按就升级 LONG_LIE。从冲击到 FALL 大约 3.5 秒，其中 2 秒是故意等的；FALL 到 LED、蜂鸣器、串口是同一个循环内，20 毫秒以内；到手机大约 1 秒。

Conclusion and limitations

What we built

  • EWMA in assembly on all six axes with separate recursive states, verified against C in the test project and at runtime.
  • Four features in physical units: mg, dps, degrees, still.
  • Five-state detector with three ordered gates: passed the logged fall, rejected 723 rows of daily and near-fall motion.
  • SOS buzzer, Telegram alert and LONG_LIE escalation added without touching the detection path.

Limitations

  • Assumes the band is worn roughly flat (Z up). Held sideways and swung hard it can misfire; a fall landing face-up with tilt under 60° is missed.
  • Thresholds tuned on two boards dropped onto cushions, not on wearers. Sitting down onto the floor without an impact is out of scope.
  • No GPS: the address is configured on the laptop.

Future work

  • Compare orientation before and after the impact instead of absolute tilt.
  • Move the relay onto the board's own Wi-Fi.
